Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 45-day move-in guarantee work?

Once 3D designs are finalized and production commences at our automated plant, we commit to handover within 45 days. If we fail to hand over on time, we pay your apartment rent for every delayed day!

What does DesignCafe's SpaceCraft™ technology include?

SpaceCraft™ is our proprietary space-saving engineering suite: hydraulic underbed storage, concealed foldaway Murphy beds, 2-in-1 coffee-cum-workstations, and corner pullout pantries that unlock up to 20% extra cubic storage.

Can I customize individual feature walls with the 3D Wall Studio?

Yes! Our 3D Wall Studio allows you to mix and match paints, fluted timber acoustic slats, exposed bricks, and Carrara marble cladding. You can also calculate the exact square-footage material cost and add it to your quotation.

Are there flexible payment and 0% EMI options available?

We partner with major leading financial institutions to provide 0% interest monthly installment plans spanning 12 to 36 months, with clear milestone-based payment disbursements.
